Get more out of every click

  • Lower actuation. Adjustable-trigger boards let you reset a key mid-press — huge for bridging and block-clutching.
  • Weight matters. Under 60g means you can flick for hours without fatigue in long Bedwars sessions.
  • Consistent clicks > fast clicks. A stable 9 CPS beats a jittery 12 when you're timing hits.
  • Test before you buy. Run this checker on any mouse or laptop trackpad you already own.
